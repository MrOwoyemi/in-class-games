Educational In-Class Games

Quick games to play together as a class or on your own. Pick a subject to see its games.

Exam boards. Computing follows the AQA GCSE Computer Science specification (8525). The other subjects aren’t matched to an exam board: they follow general curriculum topics and may not match exactly what your course requires, so check them against your own specification. This site isn’t produced or endorsed by any exam board.

    Maths game · On your own

    FizzBuzz

    Count and swap numbers for words. When a number fits one of your rules, say its word instead of the number.

    Who’s playing?

    Your rules

    2 of 5

    Pick up to five rules and give each one a word: multiples, a digit, prime numbers or square numbers.

      How to count

      Speed round

      In a speed round each number has a time limit. Run out of time and it counts as a slip.
